# Runner Automobiles PLC — DCF Valuation Model

A five-year (FY2026–FY2030) discounted cash flow valuation of Runner Automobiles PLC 
(DSE/CSE: RUNNERAUTO), Bangladesh's domestic motorcycle, three-wheeler, and (via subsidiary 
RMPLC) commercial-vehicle manufacturer.

## Overview
- **Method:** Segment-level FCFF DCF across five revenue lines (Two-Wheeler, EV, 
  Three-Wheeler, Truck/CV, Credit Business)
- **WACC:** 15.6% (CAPM-based cost of equity 25.7%, after-tax cost of debt 11.7%, 
  market-value weights 72% debt / 28% equity)
- **Terminal growth:** 3.0%
- **Scenarios:** Case 1 (Optimistic), Case 2 (Base), Case 3 (Worst) — independent 
  segment-level growth, COGS, and tax assumptions per case
- **Tax modeling:** Entity-level (RAPLC vs. RMPLC) rather than blended Group rate, 
  reflecting a verified 0% statutory tax rate on Three-Wheeler income (SRO 170-AIN/
  Income Tax/2021, confirmed in FY25 audited financial statements) against RMPLC's 
  full ~25% rate

## Key Output
| Scenario | Implied Share Price |
|---|---|
| Case 1 (Optimistic) | BDT 66 |
| Case 2 (Base) | BDT 45 |
| Case 3 (Worst) | BDT 21 |

Current price (as of 16-Jul-2026): BDT 41.6 — implying 8.2% upside under the Base Case.
